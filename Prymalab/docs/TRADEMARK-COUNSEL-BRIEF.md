# Trademark counsel brief

## Client objective

Build and potentially sell or transfer a Vietnam-first digital service that provides education, self-assessment, habit tracking and paid programs relating to nutrition, sleep quality and daily rhythms.

## Proposed/current identifiers

- Current word used: `PrymaLab Việt Nam`
- Domain: `prymalab.com`
- Exact conflicting public use: `PrymaLab` at `prymalab.net`
- Current territory: Vietnam, online
- Potential future territory: international
- Potential future products: software, education/coaching, membership, analytics; physical supplements are expressly on hold

## Questions requiring written advice

1. Does the `.net` operator or any related party own a registered, pending, state, common-law, trade-name, copyright, unfair-competition, or domain-dispute right relevant to our use?
2. What is the earliest provable use and filing date for each party and in which territories?
3. Is continued use of `PrymaLab Việt Nam` for classes 009, 035, 041, 042 or 044 defensible, and what limitations would apply?
4. Would a disclaimer, geographic modifier, coexistence agreement or scope restriction materially reduce risk?
5. Should `prymalab.com` be parked, redirected after a rebrand, transferred, or retained defensively?
6. Are there risks in our current pages mentioning that we are unrelated to `prymalab.net`?
7. What fresh name-search procedure should be used before selecting a replacement mark?
8. In whose name should applications be filed before/after forming a Vietnamese legal entity, and what assignment documents are needed?

## Evidence to preserve

- domain purchase invoice, registrar history and current account ownership;
- Git history and deployment history;
- first public screenshots and dated marketing materials;
- payment/order records showing first commercial use;
- `.net` home, about, contact, terms and product pages captured with dates;
- USPTO query record and Vietnam gateway failure record;
- founder report of Google/AI confusion.

## Deliverables requested from counsel

- signed search report and written risk opinion;
- recommended go/no-go decision for the current name;
- approved goods/services wording and filing classes;
- filing/assignment plan for Vietnam and priority markets;
- domain and coexistence strategy;
- template founder/contractor IP assignment and brand-license clauses.
