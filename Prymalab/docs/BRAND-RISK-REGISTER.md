# Brand risk register

Status: **AMBER / CONDITIONAL GO FOR VIETNAM**

Assessment date: 24 August 2026

Scope: preliminary CTO screening, not a legal clearance opinion

## Executive decision

Founder decision on 24 August 2026: continue building organic recognition for `PrymaLab Việt Nam` in Vietnam while accepting the preliminary risk described below. The operational scope is limited to digital education, self-assessment, habit tracking and lifestyle programs. A qualified trademark lawyer must still complete a professional search and provide a written opinion as soon as possible.

The lowest-risk legal path remains selecting a new, distinctive brand. If the project keeps the current name, every additional customer, backlink and piece of goodwill makes a later rebrand more expensive; this risk acceptance must therefore be reviewed immediately after counsel reports.

The domain `prymalab.com` may be retained as an evidence-preserving defensive asset while counsel advises whether it should remain parked, redirect to a new brand, or be transferred. Do not offer it to the `.net` operator, contact that operator, or make admissions without counsel.

## Why the risk is high

The marks are identical in spelling and pronunciation. The parties are not selling the same product today, but both occupy health/wellness/science territory, address sleep or metabolism, publish educational content, and trade online. The USPTO explains that goods/services can be related when they are used together, purchased by the same people, advertised together, or reasonably expected from one provider. Vietnam law also considers identical/confusing signs and related functions, utility, or distribution channels.

Adding `Việt Nam`, using a different logo, owning `.com`, or displaying a disclaimer reduces some confusion but does not create a legal safe harbor.

## Conditional release gates

Organic Vietnam-focused SEO and content may proceed. Until written clearance or rebrand completion:

- no `®` symbol or claim that the name is registered/protected;
- no paid brand ads, sponsorships, purchased backlinks, international PR campaign, app-store listing, merchandise, packaging, franchising, or licensing under `PrymaLab`;
- no supplements, peptide products, sleep compounds, melatonin, magnesium products, laboratory products, or affiliate sales;
- no expansion outside Vietnam under this name;
- preserve registration receipts, source history, invoices, designs, and dated screenshots;
- keep the public disclaimer that `prymalab.com` is independent and does not sell peptide or laboratory products;
- obtain counsel sign-off before contacting the `.net` operator.

## Required professional search

Search exact, phonetic, visual and transliterated variants across:

- Vietnam IP Office, including pending applications and Madrid designations;
- USPTO federal records, relevant state records and US common-law sources;
- WIPO Madrid Monitor and Global Brand Database;
- EUIPO/TMview and priority expansion markets;
- company/trade-name registries, social platforms, app stores, publications and domain history.

Likely Nice classes for counsel to validate include 009, 035, 041, 042 and 044. Class 005 becomes critical if any supplement or health product is contemplated.
